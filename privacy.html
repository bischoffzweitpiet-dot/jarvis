Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Jarvis is a personal desktop application run by its owner on their own computer. This policy explains what data it accesses when the owner connects their YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account.

Data accessed

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the connected channel, read the channel's basic information, video statistics and comments, and YouTube Analytics reports for those videos.
  • TikTok: basic profile information, permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account, and statistics of those videos.
  • Instagram: basic profile information, permission to publish Reels to the connected account, and insights and comments for those posts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish videos the owner's assistant created, to show the owner how those videos perform, and to improve future videos for the same account. Jarvis does not use the data for advertising and does not sell it.

Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Tokens are encrypted with Windows Data Protection. No data is sent to any server other than the platforms' own APIs and the AI services the owner configured to create video content (which receive video topics and scripts, not account data).

Sharing

Jarvis does not share account data with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Jarvis uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions), or in Instagram's settings (Apps and websites). Disconnecting an account in Jarvis deletes its stored token. Locally stored statistics can be deleted by removing Jarvis's data folder.
